Who we are

A new platform for industrialized building.

US Modular Systems is developing a Texas-based manufacturing platform for precision-engineered light-gauge steel components and modular building solutions.

Our approach brings design coordination, repeatable factory processes, quality controls and site installation planning together from the start. The goal is to help developers and contractors reduce field variability and improve delivery schedules.

  • Manufacturing-oriented design and engineering coordination
  • Standardized wall, floor and roof assemblies
  • Focus on construction efficiency and quality
  • Applications across residential, commercial and healthcare markets

What we build

Six integrated solution areas

From engineered components to future modular products, USMS is focused on systems that make projects easier to design, manufacture and assemble.

01 / WALL SYSTEMS

Prefab LGS wall panels

Exterior and interior framing panels designed for coordinated sheathing, insulation, cladding and MEP interfaces.

02 / FLOOR SYSTEMS

Floor cassettes

Engineered steel joist assemblies with structural decking and coordinated fire, acoustic and vibration performance.

03 / ROOF SYSTEMS

Steel roof framing

Prefabricated trusses and roof components designed to simplify installation and improve dimensional consistency.

04 / COMMERCIAL

Framing packages

Structural and nonstructural LGS components for retail, office, hospitality and institutional projects.

05 / HEALTHCARE

Medical building systems

Repeatable framing and future modular solutions for clinics, outpatient facilities and healthcare projects.

06 / MODULAR

Future building modules

Planned expansion opportunities include bathroom pods, kitchen pods and other integrated building modules.

Designing the complete assembly.

USMS is exploring standardized LGS assemblies that address cost, durability, energy performance, fire protection and sound isolation as a coordinated system.

  • Factory-ready exterior cladding options
  • Acoustic wall and floor solutions
  • Code-compliant, tested fire-rated assemblies
  • Coordinated service penetrations
  • Transport and erection considerations

Final systems and performance claims require design-professional review, product testing and local approvals.
